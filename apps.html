Health Applications

Mobile application categories explored in the project

Different app categories can support different aspects of health awareness. Their usefulness depends on the quality of information, design, privacy and responsible use.

🏋️

Fitness & Exercise

Step counters, activity tracking and workout plans can help users monitor movement and build consistency.

🥗

Diet & Nutrition

Nutrition apps can support food logging, hydration awareness and general nutrition education.

🧘

Mental Health

Mindfulness, breathing and mood-tracking features may support stress awareness and relaxation practices.

💊

Medicine Reminder

Reminder tools can help users organize medication times, but they do not replace medical instructions.

👨‍⚕️

Online Doctor Consultation

Teleconsultation platforms can provide access to qualified professionals where appropriate, subject to service availability.

😴

Sleep & Wellness

Sleep and wellness apps can help users observe routines, relaxation habits and general patterns.
