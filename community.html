Community Engagement

From awareness to practical digital health learning

The CEP combines community interaction, survey-based learning and practical demonstrations to connect information technology with a real-world health-awareness topic.

📝

1. Survey & Interaction

Community responses were collected using a 17-question survey covering demographics, health-app usage, useful features, challenges and expectations.

📢

2. Health Awareness Activity

Awareness discussions focused on exercise, nutrition, hydration, sleep, stress management and responsible use of health information.

💻

3. Practical Demonstration

The website demonstrates a simple health score and medicine-reminder feature to show how JavaScript and browser storage can support a student project.

🔐

4. Digital Safety Awareness

Participants were introduced to privacy, app permissions, passwords, trustworthy sources and careful sharing of health-related data.
