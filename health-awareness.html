Health Awareness

Building healthier habits with informed digital choices

Health awareness means understanding everyday practices that support physical and mental wellbeing, while knowing when professional medical advice is needed.

🏃

Physical Health

Regular movement, nutritious food, adequate hydration and sufficient sleep are important parts of everyday wellbeing.

  • Exercise regularly and choose activity appropriate for your abilities.
  • Prefer balanced meals with varied food groups.
  • Drink water regularly and pay attention to your body's needs.
  • Aim for a consistent sleep routine and adequate rest.
🧠

Mental Wellness

Mental wellness includes managing stress, taking breaks and maintaining supportive social connections.

  • Practice stress-management techniques such as breathing or mindfulness.
  • Maintain a healthy balance between study/work and rest.
  • Talk to trusted people when you need support.
  • Seek qualified professional help for persistent or serious concerns.
📱

Digital Health Awareness

Mobile apps can support tracking and learning, but information should be evaluated carefully.

  • Prefer information from credible health organizations or qualified professionals.
  • Check dates, sources and evidence before acting on health claims.
  • Do not treat app scores or automated advice as a diagnosis.
🔐

Safe Use of Health Applications

Privacy and security are part of responsible digital health use.

  • Review app permissions and only grant access that is necessary.
  • Use strong passwords and keep apps and devices updated.
  • Read privacy policies before sharing sensitive information.
  • Be cautious with advertisements, paid claims and unverified recommendations.
Educational note: This website is for educational and health-awareness purposes only. It is not a medical diagnosis or treatment system. For medical concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
